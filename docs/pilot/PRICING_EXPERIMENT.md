# Pricing experiment: US$149 hypothesis

No customer, conversion, revenue, or willingness-to-pay evidence is recorded. Treat $149 as a test price only.

For the first five qualified conversations, offer the same one-repository, 30-day, read-only scope at $149 and record, with permission: qualification, whether a proposal was requested, acceptance/decline, stated reason, actual delivery hours, direct cost, and payment status. Do not vary scope and price simultaneously. This small convenience sample cannot establish market price. Review after five conversations or 60 days, whichever comes first.

Outbound automation is disabled. Human review and approval are required before proposals or messages are sent.
