# Illustrative unit economics (assumptions only)

At the US$149 price hypothesis:

| Item | Assumption | Amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Price | test hypothesis | $149.00 |
| Operator labor | 2.5 hours × $40/hour | $100.00 |
| Direct tools/compute | placeholder estimate | $5.00 |
| Contribution before overhead/tax | price less labor and direct costs | $44.00 |
| Contribution margin | $44 ÷ $149 | 29.5% |

Planning assumptions, not measured costs or a profitability claim. Excludes payment fees, sales time, insurance, taxes, refunds, support, hosting, and rework. Replace assumptions with actuals before pricing decisions. Offline tasks meter $0 provider cost because no provider is configured; infrastructure cost is unknown.
