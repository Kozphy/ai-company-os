# Read-only GitHub CI Reliability Review — scope template

**Status:** Draft template; not an offer or contract. **Indicative price hypothesis:** US$149 for one repository and an agreed 30-day window. Scope and price require human approval.

## Included

- Read-only inspection of one authorized GitHub repository's Actions history for up to 30 days.
- Summary of observed successful/failed runs and supported failure categories.
- Findings linked to available run/job evidence, with unknowns called out.
- Markdown and JSON report drafts for customer review.

## Excluded

- Code changes, patch execution, merge/deploy actions, secrets, private source-code review, incident response, SLA, compliance/security certification, and guarantees of future CI performance.

## Inputs and access

Customer identifies the repository, confirms authority, approves the time window, and supplies a least-privilege read-only mechanism or export. Do not send credentials by email or include secrets in exports. Work starts only after written authorization and access validation.

## Delivery and acceptance

Draft report is delivered through a customer-approved channel after human review. Customer may identify factual corrections within five business days. Payment terms, taxes, cancellation, retention, and governing terms require legal/business review before use.
